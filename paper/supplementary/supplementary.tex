\noindent This supplement contains the three tables that the main text refers to as
Tables~S1--S3. Table~S1 characterizes the three workload sources. Table~S2 counts, per
source and per discounting rule, how often the rule beats both the full-trust and the
network-blind extremes. Table~S3 reports the one-factor-at-a-time sensitivity of the
per-channel rule. The tables are provided here, rather than in the main text, only to keep
the main manuscript within the target length; they are not additional experiments.

\section*{S1. Load characterization}

\begin{table}[h]
\centering
\caption{Load characterization per source (mean over segments). “CV(1s)” is the coefficient of
variation of per-second arrival counts; “AC(1s)” the lag-1 autocorrelation of that series;
“rate chg.” the mean relative change of the 5-second windowed arrival rate across a 5-second
shift; “Fano” the variance-to-mean ratio of per-second counts. A stationary Poisson process
would show CV $\approx 0$, AC $\approx 0$ and Fano $\approx 1$.}
